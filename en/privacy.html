Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026 | Artcoder Sistemas e Tecnologia

ARTCODER SISTEMAS E TECNOLOGIA ("Artcoder", "we", "our"), registered and headquartered in Curitiba - PR, Brazil, is the owner and developer of the AirsoftHub platform and mobile application. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when using our website and services.

By using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Personal Data Collected

We collect data necessary to properly provide our services, including:

  • Registration Data: Full name, email address, profile photo/avatar, and nickname/callsign.
  • User and Operation Data: Information about your gear (loadouts) and preferences registered on the platform.
  • Location Data (Geolocation): When authorized by you, we collect precise or approximate geographic location data to enable searching for nearby Airsoft fields, groups, and matches.
  • Device and Log Data: IP address, operating system, device version, and access logs for account security.

2. Purpose of Data Use

The collected data is used for the following purposes:

  • Enable registration, login, and management of your user profile.
  • Display matches, communities, and organized fields based on your geolocation.
  • Manage attendance lists, match confirmations, and evaluations between players and organizers (peer review).
  • Process subscription management and account features.
  • Ensure platform security, prevent fraud, and provide technical support via artcoder@artcoder.com.br.

3. Data Sharing

Artcoder does not sell your personal data. Sharing occurs strictly in the following scenarios:

  • Other Users and Organizers: Your public name, callsign, photos, and match attendance status are visible to other participants and administrators of the events you choose to join.
  • Infrastructure Providers: Secure cloud data storage under strict security and encryption standards.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law, court order, or competent authority.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers with strict access control and encrypted communication (HTTPS/TLS). We implement technical measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your personal data will be retained as long as your account remains active. You may request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data at any time:

  1. Through the App: By accessing your profile settings and selecting the "Delete My Account" option.
  2. Via Email: By sending a formal request to artcoder@artcoder.com.br or accessing our Account Deletion Request page.

After confirmation, your profile data and personal history will be permanently removed from our active database, except where retention is required by legal obligation.

6. Data Subject Rights (LGPD)

Under the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD - Law No. 13.709/2018), you have the right to confirm the existence of processing, access or correct incomplete or outdated data, and request anonymization, deletion, or revocation of consent for the use of your location.

7. Data Protection Officer (DPO) Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights as a data subject, please contact us through our official support channel:
